<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Cyberpunk Minimalism
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **High Contrast & Neon Accents**: Deep, void-like backgrounds punctuated by intense, glowing neon accents to signify data and energy.
  2. **Data as Art**: Treating charts, graphs, and data streams not just as information, but as primary aesthetic elements.
  3. **Fluidity & Motion**: Everything should feel alive and flowing, mimicking the constant movement of markets.
  4. **Transparency & Depth**: Extensive use of glassmorphism and layering to create a sense of depth and complexity without clutter.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Void Black (#050505)**: The base canvas, representing the unknown/market depth.
  - **Electric Blue (#00F0FF)**: Primary accent for positive signals and key actions, representing intelligence and clarity.
  - **Signal Red (#FF2A6D)**: Secondary accent for alerts and critical data, representing urgency and risk.
  - **Holographic Silver (#E0E0E0)**: For text and structural elements, reflecting a metallic, high-tech feel.
  - *Intent*: To evoke a feeling of being inside a sophisticated trading terminal from the future, where clarity emerges from darkness.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetric Grid**: Breaking away from standard 12-column layouts. Use overlapping sections and off-center focal points to create dynamic tension.
  - **HUD-Inspired Interfaces**: Floating panels and data overlays that feel like a Heads-Up Display.
  - **Vertical Rhythm**: Strong vertical lines and scrolling typography to guide the eye down the page.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Glowing Edges**: Subtle, pulsing borders on cards and inputs.
  - **Glitch Effects**: Micro-interactions on hover that simulate digital interference or data processing.
  - **Floating Particles**: Background ambient animation of connecting nodes or data points.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Instant Feedback**: Every click and hover should have an immediate, tactile visual response (glow, color shift, sound).
  - **Precision Control**: Sliders, toggles, and inputs should feel weighted and precise, like high-end audio equipment.

  **Animation**:
  - **Smooth Easing**: All transitions should use custom cubic-bezier curves for a "magnetic" feel.
  - **Sequential Reveals**: Content should cascade in, rather than appearing all at once.
  - **Parallax Depth**: Background elements moving at different speeds to enhance the 3D effect.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: *Orbitron* (Geometric Sans) - Bold, wide tracking, uppercase for impact.
  - **Body**: *Rajdhani* (Squared Sans) - Technical, legible, slightly condensed for data density.
  - **Monospace**: *JetBrains Mono* - For code snippets and numerical data, reinforcing the algorithmic nature.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Brutalist Tech
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Raw Functionality**: Exposing the structure of the design—grids, lines, and raw data.
  2. **Monochromatic with Impact**: Using a strictly limited palette to force focus on content and structure.
  3. **Bold Typography**: Type as the primary image. Huge, screen-filling headlines.
  4. **Grid Adherence**: A visible, rigid grid that organizes chaos.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Carbon Grey (#121212)**: A flat, matte background.
  - **Terminal Green (#00FF00)**: The classic phosphor green, used sparingly for critical data and active states.
  - **Warning Orange (#FF5500)**: For alerts and calls to action.
  - **Concrete White (#E0E0E0)**: For text, slightly muted to reduce eye strain.
  - *Intent*: To appeal to the hardcore trader and developer who values raw speed and data over "pretty" visuals.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Modular Grid**: A strict bento-box style layout where every element fits into a defined rectangular space.
  - **Split Screen**: Dividing the screen into clear functional zones (e.g., Navigation | Content | Data).
  - **Sticky Headers/Footers**: Persistent toolbars that anchor the experience.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Visible Grid Lines**: Thin lines separating all content areas.
  - **Monospace Everything**: Using code-style fonts for more than just code.
  - **Marquee Text**: Scrolling text banners for news and updates.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Mechanical**: Clicks should feel instant and mechanical, like flipping a physical switch.
  - **No-Nonsense**: Transitions are instant (0ms) or very fast (100ms). No fading or sliding.

  **Animation**:
  - **Blinking Cursors**: To suggest a live terminal environment.
  - **Step Changes**: Values that snap to new numbers rather than counting up.
  - **Glitch Transitions**: Hard cuts between states with occasional digital artifacts.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: *Druk Wide* or *Clash Display* - Heavy, imposing, and structural.
  - **Body**: *IBM Plex Mono* - A highly legible monospace font that works well for text and code.
  - **Data**: *Share Tech Mono* - Specifically for numerical data display.
</idea>
